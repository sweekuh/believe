# Seasons 1 and 2 season review (2026-10-12)

This is the same whole-season pass that Seasons 3 and 4 had (`s3-season-review.md`,
`s4-season-review.md`), run on the two oldest seasons. Every changed card carries a
`Season review 2026-10-12:` line in its `groundingNotes`.

## Method

- Every card was read end to end.
- Seven researchers rebuilt each episode's beats from fan transcripts (Forever Dreaming, plus
  Springfield for some episodes), Wikipedia and recaps.
- Each researcher fact-checked every card and ran a commentary pass.
- Most Season 1 and 2 cards had been verified from search-result snippets only ("no primary
  page openable this session"). This pass opened the pages and transcripts.
- Reddit was not attempted, and the Fandom wiki was blocked.

**One structural constraint.** S1 and S2 episodes show every card at once, with no fold. So the
pass replaced weak or wrong cards rather than piling on new ones. Every episode stays at six cards
or fewer.

## Structural fixes

- **Every S1/S2 episode now has a chronological order**, except the Pilot.
  - Before this pass, 15 of the 22 episodes had no order at all, so pages fell back to paste
    order.
  - Two of the ordered ones were wrong: S1E8 put the darts match first, and S2E3 put the
    jersey protest first.
- **The Pilot is the deliberate exception.** It stays unordered so it keeps the approved
  prototype's sign-first reading order (`design-reference/believe-s1e1.html`). It is the first
  page the app opens on, and the BELIEVE sign is the app's name.
- **The five Pilot cards** were hand-written from the prototype and had no category, grounding
  or interest. All five now have them.

## Cardinal-rule fixes (attributions)

- **S1E1 press conference.** The card credited Camus with choosing hope. In *The Myth of
  Sisyphus*, Camus calls the absurd "the contrary of hope".
  - It now cites G. K. Chesterton, *Heretics* (1905), ch. 12: hope means hoping when things are
    hopeless. That was checked in the primary text.
  - *Ted Lasso and Philosophy* has a matching chapter, ch. 16.
- **S1E8 darts.** The Whitman misattribution is now sourced to the co-director of the Walt
  Whitman Archive (via Snopes) and to Wikiquote.
  - The wrong book citation and the wrong back-reference were removed.
  - Ted says he saw the line **painted on a wall**. Five Season 3 and 4 cards described "the sign
    over the dartboard"; all five are fixed.
- **S2E7 Steinem.** The card said the show credits Steinem. Sharon names nobody on screen.
- **Wrong chapter citations to *Ted Lasso and Philosophy*** were removed from S1E1 (Rebecca's
  revenge), S1E4 (accountability) and S1E8 (darts).

  The owner confirmed this.

  and do not rename it.

  - It placed the darts scene in the finale.
